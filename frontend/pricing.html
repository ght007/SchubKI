Questions

What is the difference between the General and the Expert view?

Both show the same run. The General view is one compact page for sales and project leads: problems in the request, what to test first, questions for the customer and the first experiment. The Expert view adds the full report, the method comparison, every source quote with its highlighted page, the verification and quality checks, and the model trace.

Can I change plans later?

Yes. Switch between Basic and Pro on this page at any time; your saved enquiries and run history stay in your account.
